# Contributing

This repo is a personal 12-week system-design study curriculum. Contributions are welcome if they improve accuracy, clarity, or fill in a week that hasn't been expanded yet.

## Ground rules

- Follow the editorial standard in `REVIEW-NOTES.md` for any lesson or design-lab content — it defines the required sections (Goal, Mental model, Core explanation, Concrete example, Architecture diagram, Tradeoff, Failure mode, Practical exercise, Retrieval questions, Exit criterion, Primary sources, Deep-dive reading).
- Don't expand a week's scope beyond what `ROADMAP.md` promises for that week. Topics explicitly deferred (see `REVIEW-NOTES.md`) should stay deferred until their scheduled week.
- Cite verifiable primary sources (RFCs, official docs, production engineering writeups), not just secondary explainers.
- Keep `PROGRESS.md` checkboxes accurate — only mark a day complete once its content actually exists and has been reviewed.
- Don't spoil `answer-key.md` content inside lesson files; it's meant to be opened only after the corresponding review quiz.

## Workflow

1. Open an issue or PR describing the change (new week, correction, added sources, etc.).
2. Keep PRs scoped to one week or one topic where possible.
3. If adding a new week, mirror the structure of `week-01-foundations/` (`README.md`, `day-0N-*.md`, `cheat-sheet.md`, `resources.md`, `answer-key.md`).
